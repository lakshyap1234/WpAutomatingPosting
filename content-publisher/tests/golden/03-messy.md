# The Hidden Cost of "Free" Software

Everyone loves free tools. But "free" rarely means zero cost — it usually means the cost moved somewhere else.

Key things we look at before adopting a free tool:

- Who maintains it, and how often it's updated
- Whether we can export our data
- What happens if the company behind it disappears
