For years we planned in monthly cycles. It felt efficient: one big meeting, one big document, and then everyone went off to execute. In practice, by week two the plan was already out of date.

THE PROBLEM

Monthly plans assumed we knew what the month would bring. We never did. Client requests, bugs, and hiring all arrived on their own schedule.

WHAT WE CHANGED

  1. Every Monday, a 30-minute planning call.
  2. Each person commits to three outcomes, not tasks.
  3. Friday, a short written recap of what shipped and what slipped, and why.

The results after one quarter: fewer missed deadlines, and far less time spent re-planning in the middle of the month. See the template we use at www.example.com/weekly-template.
