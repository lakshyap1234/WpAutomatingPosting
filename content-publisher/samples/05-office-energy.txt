

How We Cut Our Office Electricity Bill by 30% This Summer
Written by Ahmed Al-Balushi
Last updated: 14 September 2026



Every June our electricity bill jumps, and every June we tell ourselves
we'll "look into it." This year we actually did — and the results
surprised us.   
Here's the short version:
Cooling was 70% of the bill
Most of it ran when nobody was in the office
Three cheap changes fixed most of it

It worked.

WHERE THE ENERGY WAS GOING
We borrowed a plug-in energy meter and logged usage for two weeks. The
pattern was obvious once we saw it: the AC units ran at full power
from 6am to 11pm, even though the office is empty after 6pm.
Other culprits we found:
• Two old fridges in the pantry (one was completely empty)
• Monitors left on standby over the weekend
* The server-room AC, set to 18°C "just to be safe"
- Printers that never go to sleep

What we changed:
1) Put every AC unit on a timer: on at 7:30am, off at 6pm, and
off entirely on Fridays and Saturdays.
2) Raised the thermostat from 21°C to 24°C. After the first week,
nobody noticed.
3) Unplugged the spare fridge & replaced the other with an efficient model.
4) Set monitors and printers to sleep after 15 minutes.

Note: we checked the server room with our IT provider before touching it. Don't skip that step — some equipment really does need cooler air.


The Results
July's bill was 31% lower than last July's, even though it was a hotter month. The timers cost less than 60 rials in total and paid for themselves in under three weeks.
